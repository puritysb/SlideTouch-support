Privacy Policy

2026-09-13

Document editing runs locally on your Mac. We do not upload document contents or include advertising or analytics SDKs.

The app accesses files you choose through Open or drag and drop. OCR uses Apple Vision; inpainting and segmentation use bundled Core ML models on your Mac. Original files are preserved.

Optional one-time developer tips use Apple’s App Store and StoreKit and require network access. Apple handles payments under its privacy policy. We do not receive payment card details. The app verifies transactions without sending them to a developer server. Apple provides sales and financial reports. Editing and export remain free.

Preferences, recent file references and review-request eligibility are stored locally. Eligibility includes first-use/request dates, app version and up to two hashed file paths. Document contents are not included in these records. Review requests use Apple’s standard interface; Apple decides whether to display them. A Help menu link lets you open the App Store review page.

Exported files stay in the locations you choose. Removing the app does not necessarily delete exported files or all macOS container data. You can remove local app data separately through macOS.

For privacy questions, contact puritysb@gmail.com.
