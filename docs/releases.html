Release notes

Updates and version history

Status checked September 13, 2026

Version 1.1.0 is released. Version 1.2.0 was submitted on September 13, 2026 and is awaiting App Review. The 1.2 demos show the submitted version; those changes become available after release.

1.2.0 · Awaiting App Review

  • Find editing tools with visible labels and a clearer editing workflow.
  • Compare larger before-and-after previews. Adjust restoration bounds and review texture-preserving corrections before applying them.
  • Improve OCR selection consistency when undoing and redoing text edits.
  • Export from the File menu; Command-Shift-E opens PDF export. Unfinished text edits must be finalized or cancelled before export.
  • Add Simplified and Traditional Chinese interfaces alongside English, Korean and Japanese.
  • Offer an optional, repeatable one-time developer tip in Help or Settings. Editing and export remain free; tips unlock no features and are not subscriptions.

1.1.0 · Released

  • Expanded OCR language choices, including Italian, Dutch, Polish, Turkish and Arabic. Recognition availability depends on Apple Vision and macOS.
  • Improved text recognition, background restoration and object-selection performance.
  • Load image-processing models when needed and release them when idle.

1.0 · 2026-02-11

Initial Mac App Store release: OCR text editing, background restoration, object selection and movement, text styling and shapes, layer-based undo/redo, and PDF, image, video and GIF export.
